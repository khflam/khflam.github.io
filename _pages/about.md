I am a PhD candidate at the University of Oxford, supervised by **Yee Whye Teh** and **George Deligiannidis**. My research focuses on Sampling, Optimisation, and Generative Modelling. Prior to my PhD, I spent six years in machine learning engineering, data science and analytics, and statistical modelling roles across retail and financial services, while earning my master's degrees in Mathematics and Information Technology.


Research Interests
======
My primary research interests lie in developing numerical methods for sampling and optimisation of probabilistic models. My past work has extended **Predictor-Corrector samplers** for Diffusion Models, improved training with variance-reduced **Reparameterisation Gradients**, and generalising Generalised Linear Models by learning link functions with **Invertible Networks**.
